Laddoo Dash

Collect laddoos. Dodge chilies. Jump hurdles.

    Keys: arrows or A and D to move, Up, W or Space to jump, P to pause. On a phone, use the buttons or swipe on the game (up to jump). Laddoo = 10 points, golden = 50. Every 3 laddoos in a row raises your combo up to x5. Hurdles are jumped, walls are dodged. Pick up a shield, magnet or x2. Every 10 laddoos is a new level and the world changes every 3 levels. Every 5th level has a boss. Your top 5 scores, unlocked characters and daily mission are saved on this device.
