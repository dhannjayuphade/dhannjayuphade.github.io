Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Dhannjay Hub (“we”, “us”, this website at dhannjayuphade.github.io) collects when you create an account, why we collect it, and the control you have over it. It is written to follow India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025.

1. Who is responsible

The website is run by Dhannjay Uphade, an individual creator in India. Contact for all privacy matters: dhannjayuphade5@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

We collect only what is needed to run your account:

  • Name and email address (given by you, or by your Google account if you use Google login).
  • Profile photo and bio, only if you add them.
  • Login information: login method (email or Google), whether your email is verified, the time of your last logins, your total number of logins, and the type of device you used (for example “Android • Chrome”).
  • Password: if you sign up with email, your password is handled by Firebase Authentication. We never see or store it in readable form.

We do not collect your phone number, city, address, location, contacts or any payment details.

3. Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you log in on one or more devices.
  • To show your profile and, if the admin has granted it, a verified (blue tick) badge.
  • To let the site owner see who has an account, so the site can be managed and abuse can be stopped.

We do not sell your data, show ads based on it, or use it to profile you.

4. Who can see your data

  • You can see and edit your own profile.
  • The site owner (admin) can see the name, email, photo, last login and login history of all users in a private admin panel.
  • No other user can see your account data.
  • Service providers: your data is stored and processed by Google Firebase (Google LLC), and the website is hosted on GitHub Pages. These providers may process data on servers outside India.
  • We may disclose data if the law or a court order requires it.

5. Cookies and device storage

We use your browser’s local and session storage only to keep you logged in, to remember a random device ID (so you can see your logged-in devices), and to remember small settings. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, photo, login history and login are erased from our database. Short-term technical logs kept by Google or GitHub follow their own policies.

7. Your rights

Under Indian law you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you. Most of it is visible on your profile page.
  • Correct or update your name, photo and bio from your profile page.
  • Erase your data: use Delete my account and data on your profile page. It is permanent.
  • Withdraw consent at any time. Deleting your account does this. It is as easy as giving consent.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to.
  • Grieve: write to us at the email above. We will reply within 90 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. How to report a problem or make a request

To see, correct or delete your data, report a privacy concern, or complain about misuse of your data, send an email to dhannjayuphade5@gmail.com from the email address of your account. Please write Privacy request in the subject and describe what you want. We will confirm receipt and reply within 90 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Children

This website is meant for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly create accounts for children. If you believe a child under 18 has registered, write to us and we will delete the account.

10. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS and protected by Firebase security rules so that each user can read and change only their own record. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affecting your data occurs, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board as required by law.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will update the date above and, where required, ask for your consent again.

By creating an account you confirm that you have read this policy and consent to the use of your data as described. You may withdraw this consent at any time by deleting your account.
